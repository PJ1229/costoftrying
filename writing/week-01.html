Week 01 — Learning Log

Ok, so…

How do I even start?

I guess I can lay out some of my goals.

Goals

I want to make a short film about myself and my relationships (romantic for now). I want to reflect deeply on my life, and I want to grow from reflection. I want to be vulnerable, but artistic. I want to share parts of my life. I also don’t want to reveal parts that are too private.

What I mean by this is:

  • I won’t use real names of people
  • I won’t use real quotes from people
  • Probably more rules that I’ll expand upon in the future

I want to write creatively. I want to learn how to use my words to tell a story. I don’t want to tell by telling, I want to tell by creating.

The main goal of this is to create a single 15–20 minute (actual length is to be determined during editing) film about a section of my life. I want to wrap it around an adventure/trip that I will film. I will also include clips from my life in general. While I’m creating this, I want to share poems/short stories of my life to build the brand of the film.

I believe I can use these smaller sections (short videos, poems, etc) to build up hype, incentive me to continue to work on it, and build a brand around the film. (As I learned from my startup distribution over everything XD)

Another goal is to be public about the entire process. I want to document the failures, the successes, and the journey primarily on social media but also within the communities I’m in.

Scope

Ok sweet. Now that I have some goals, let’s narrow the scope.

I am probably going to plan a trip to somewhere scenic and film the entire thing. I’m not sure If it’ll be by myself or with others yet. I will then tell the story of a relationship while intertwined with telling the story of the trip.

Realistically I can’t tell the story of EVERY relationship I’ve been in throughout my ENTIRE life. I honestly probably can’t even tell the story of one relationship the way I want to. I also can’t remember a lot of the small details of my relationships.

What I can remember very vividly is the FEELINGS of moments. I can pinpoint these feelings to parts of the trip.

Another thought is to have actors and actresses, but this is likely a project for another time.

Weeklys

Now that we have a scope, how can I achieve this?

Realistically, I don’t have a lot of time per week to commit to this, so I will probably work on it throughout the entire semester (even through the summer if necessary).

Here are my goal weekly deliverables that I CAN commit to:

  • 1 piece of writing such as: brain dumps, storyboarding, trip planning, or scripting
  • 1 creative piece such as: cinematography work, video edit, poems, design, photography
  • Post these on Instagram, TikTok, Youtube, and the Website (costoftrying.com)
