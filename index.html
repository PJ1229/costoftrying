it's always been easy to determine what I want to do with my life, and that's to connect with people, connect people, and tell their stories.

i see this page as the entirety of my creative endeavors. i want to be able to align all my creative work with this project. i think a lot of my struggle in creative work is finding direction and making sure the tree is well trimmed. what i mean by this is cutting off branches that don't fit the shape i envision or that stunt the growth of the tree.

right now i'm in three builder communities: v1, shift, and the midnight club. costoftrying is how i amplify them, and how i learn to build my own community around this philosophy.

the philosophy

anyone gets a space

I believe that literally anyone should be able to go to a midnight club and start working towards the goals they have.

especially those who are more shy and have less faith in their goals.

creative mobility

centered around providing creative mobility to everyone. Generally, people who want to try something new should be able to have the faith that they can do it.

anti-instrumentalist

I suggest taking on an anti-instrumentalist philosophy.

We want our culture to reflect a group where people participate for the sake of belief and enjoyment of their idea rather than to get something out of it.

the means are just as important as, if not more important than, the ends.

the means are themselves valuable.

no ego

We don't want positions in the club and we want an expressed lack of ego.
