what we're building

some of the most interesting people are already out there, in your school, your city, your community, with projects they've been sitting on and passions they haven't had the time to explore. they just haven't found each other yet.

we help existing communities create the space for that to happen. through video and storytelling, events, and growth marketing when a story is ready to find its people, we amplify what's already there, connecting the creative and technical people who are ready to finally try something.

what's next.

we're always looking for communities to work with. if you're building something and want to tell its story or create more space for your people, reach out.
