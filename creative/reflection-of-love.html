Reflection of Love

There’s a part of you that never left

I still have the letters you wrote me as we went off to college, and

I still have the sugar chapstick you left in my car on our first date

A lot of me is ashamed to admit that I’ll never forget

but forgetting doesn’t mean moving on

Moving on means being content with the silent intimacy of sharing moments that no one else will ever have

Being happy but not in the joyous way that brings a smile to your face.

Being happy in the way that gives you satisfaction in that you’ve tried.

I’m now 20

3 years removed from saying the words “I love you”

but for the first time I’m learning what love actually is

it’s not an explosion that hits you all at once

it’s a small fire that needs protection from the gusts of air and the rain

but eventually it grows so tall that even the harshest storm won’t blow it out

Sometimes it grows too hot that it burns

But it’ll always be there to keep you warm.
