Ocean Eyes

“why are you scared of the ocean”

i ask the girl i met 3 days ago

her eyes twinkle like the sun hitting the sea

“because i’m scared i might drown”

she tells me

“but what if i can teach you how to swim?”

i say as her blue eyes open wide,

letting me dive in

and as i gasp for air, i realize

that there’s a tsunami inside

and maybe, just maybe, i’m not meant to survive
