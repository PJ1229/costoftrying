# costoftrying: layout requirements

Use this when editing `index.html`, `retreat.html`, `styles.css`, or copy.

## Punctuation

- Do not use Unicode em dashes (U+2014) anywhere in site copy, meta text, UI strings, or comments. Use commas, periods, colons, or ASCII hyphens instead.

## Voice

- The page is in PJ's lowercase voice. Quotes stay verbatim, including his capitalization and typos.
- Do not invent facts, dates, or numbers.
- Do not add unreleased or private events.
- Do not restore the fall Sunday sessions line.
- Do not add partner logos or a partner section. v1, shift, and tmc appear only as plain text in his sentences.
- One small photo per event in the archive. No hero image.

## Typography

One body tier for prose, captions, and the email note: 17px, line-height 1.62, weight 400, color `rgba(17, 17, 17, 0.6)`.

- Hero thesis (`h1`): smaller than a poster, `#111`, weight 600, tight line-height. The sentence is long, so it stays readable.
- Section titles (`h2`): smaller than the thesis, `#111`, weight 600, no uppercase.
- Labels (`h3`): same size as body, `#111`, weight 600.
- Font: Inter, then Helvetica Neue, Arial.

## Files

- `index.html` at the site root
- `retreat.html` for the tmc retreat
- `styles.css`
- `CNAME` stays `costoftrying.com`
- Photos live in `images/`. The page shows `tmc-retreat-a.jpg` and `unsanctioned-still.jpg` only.
